POEM

The origin of the Ozymandias name, which is what I go by.

A. The name

000100*WHO: Ozymandias is the Greek name for the Egyptian pharaoh Ramesses II.
000200*SOURCE: The Greek historian Diodorus Siculus described a huge Egyptian statue and its inscription in his Bibliotheca historica. That passage is what the poems of 1818 drew on.

B. What it is about

000300*A traveller describes a shattered statue in the desert. The king's boast of power is still on the pedestal, and nothing else is left around it.
000400*It is usually read as a poem about how power and empire fade, while time and the sculptor's craft outlast them.
