ATLANTIS AND MODEL BUILDERS

Who built the Atlantis, in which country, and what is still in production. Rivendell does not publish a complete record of builders, release counts or end dates, so most of this is owner-forum recollection and some cells say Not documented.

A. Atlantis by generation

Sources give no exact years for each generation, so the years are approximate.

SEQGENBUILDER (COUNTRY)APPROX. YEARSWHAT DEFINED IT
0001001Toyo (Japan)Announced 1999; first frames due April 2000Based on the Waterford-built All Rounder. S-shaped chainstays, pointy head-tube lugs, mostly flat top tube, short stays. 26 in. or 700c.
0002002Toyo (Japan)Not documentedOne-curve (J-shaped) chainstays, slightly slacker head angle, more tire clearance, rounder lugs, more braze-ons.
0003003Waterford (USA)About 2013 to 2017 (forum recollection)Stepped in when Toyo sourcing got hard. Slightly longer stays; some small sizes on 650B.
0004004Taiwan ("MIT")About 2018 to nowMuch longer chainstays and wheelbase, sloping top tube, more tire clearance, 650B and 700c.
000500*NEWEST FRAME: The 2025 Taiwan frame takes a 27.2 mm post and up to 2.3 in. tires. Rivendell says it is built in the same shop as the Appaloosa. Earlier frames mostly take 26.8 mm posts; very old ones take 27.2 mm.
000600*KNOWN RELEASES: The 2025 batch (ETA mid-April 2025) sold out. A next batch was listed for July/August 2026. A new presale is listed for 11/24 at noon Pacific with frames due early January; the page does not show the year, so check rivbike.com.
000700*NOT DOCUMENTED: Exact batch counts for the Toyo, Waterford and early Taiwan eras. Forum members said Nobilette did not build Atlantis frames.
000800*NAME NOTE: One member believes the "2" on some seat tubes marks the name's second use, because Bridgestone used "Atlantis" earlier.

C. Number of releases per model

Nothing found counts releases by model, so no numbers are given. These are the patterns the sources show.

003700*BATCH-BASED: Rivendell works in presale batches. The Atlantis and Appaloosa alternate years, and the Sam, Roadini and Yellowolley have recent presales.
003800*MODELS THAT RETURN: Owners say models often come back after gaps, so a missing year does not mean retirement.
003900*ONE-OFFS: The Roscoe Bubbe was a one-time run to use up spare forks, and the Rosco Platypus was a limited run.

D. What would fill the gaps

004000*READER AND CYCLOFIEND: Rivendell Reader issues give announcement dates, and Cyclofiend has per-model pages.
004100*OWNERS' SHEET: The owners' group keeps an editable spreadsheet, though it does not track builders or end dates.
004200*RIVENDELL ITSELF: The most reliable route is to ask directly; staff reply to emails about specific models.
